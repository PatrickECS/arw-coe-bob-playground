# Arrow Experience Center

Let's leave the best effort approach of past and step in to a more professional setup and operated environment.

## Welcome to the Arrow Experience Center

The Arrow Experience Center is a shared technical environment where Arrow, technology vendors and channel partners work together to turn complex ideas into practical, validated solutions.

Modern customer requirements rarely fit within a single product or vendor portfolio. Infrastructure, platforms, networking, security, automation and AI increasingly need to work together as part of a complete architecture. The Experience Center provides the environments, specialist knowledge and collaborative framework needed to explore these connections before a solution is introduced into production.

The global Experience Center model is supported by two regional foundations: Stockholm for EMEA and Atlanta for North America. Each region provides infrastructure, technical expertise and access to Arrow's technology ecosystem, while following a shared approach to solution development, validation and demonstration.

### EMEA Experience Center

Based in Stockholm, the central datacenter provides a shared technical capability for Arrow teams and partners across Europe, the Middle East and Africa.

The centre brings together regional infrastructure, specialist expertise and vendor technologies in an environment designed for demonstrations, proofs of concept, technical workshops and solution validation. It allows capabilities developed in one country to be shared across the region, reducing duplication and making specialist knowledge more accessible.

The EMEA model connects local Arrow teams and partners with regional experts and Centres of Excellence. It supports collaboration across countries while retaining the local relationships and market knowledge needed for effective customer engagement.

### North America Experience Center

Based in Atlanta, the North America Experience Center provides a shared technical capability for Arrow teams and partners across the region.

The centre combines infrastructure, specialist expertise and vendor technologies to support demonstrations, proofs of concept, technical workshops and solution validation. It gives partners access to environments and capabilities that may be difficult to build and maintain independently.

The North America model connects Arrow teams, partners and technology specialists around defined customer requirements. It supports the development of practical, repeatable solutions and enables technical knowledge and demonstrations to be reused across the region.

## How the Experience Center Can Help

Arrow teams and partners can use the Experience Center to:

- Develop and demonstrate multi-vendor solutions
- Validate architectures and technical integrations
- Build proofs of concept for defined customer opportunities
- Test technologies in realistic scenarios
- Run technical workshops and enablement sessions
- Create reusable demonstrations and reference architectures
- Identify risks, dependencies and integration gaps earlier

## A Purposeful Engagement Model

The Experience Center is not simply a showroom or an unrestricted test environment. Every engagement begins with a defined business or technical objective.

The appropriate location, specialists, platforms and delivery format are selected according to the required outcome. An engagement may include a demonstration, technical workshop, architecture review, solution validation or structured proof of concept.

Across EMEA and North America, the Experience Center connects Arrow specialists, vendor experts, regional teams and channel partners. Together, they can test assumptions, demonstrate how technologies work together and build credible solutions around real customer requirements.

The result is a practical path from technical question to validated outcome, helping partners reduce uncertainty, strengthen customer conversations and move forward with greater confidence.
