# Environment

## Purpose

The Arrow Experience Center (AEC) is a customer-facing demonstration and validation environment. It enables customers and partners to explore, test, and validate technology solutions before production adoption.

## Overall environment

- Location: Arrow Experience Center
- Platform: IBM Fusion on-prem
- Primary use: Demonstrations, proofs of concept, and technical validation
- Audience: IBM, Arrow, customers, and partners

## Scope

This environment supports containerized workloads and AI-assisted software engineering demonstrations, including IBM Bob running on IBM Fusion on-prem.

## Out of scope

- Production customer workloads
- Long-term data storage
- Customer production deployments
